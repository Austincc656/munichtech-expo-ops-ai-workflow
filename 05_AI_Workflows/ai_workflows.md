# AI Workflow Concepts

## AI Exhibitor Onboarding
Automated onboarding workflow for exhibitors including branding collection, booth setup tracking, and missing document reminders.

## AI Speaker Coordination
AI-assisted speaker reminders, presentation tracking, and session readiness monitoring.

## AI Attendee Matchmaking
Recommendation engine suggesting networking opportunities based on attendee interests and startup ecosystem relevance.

## AI FAQ Chatbot
AI-powered support assistant for attendees, sponsors, and exhibitors.

## AI Sponsor Reporting
Automated sponsor reporting using operational data and engagement analytics.
